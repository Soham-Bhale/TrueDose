About LogicLens

Welcome to LogicLens, your personal code debugging assistant designed to bring clarity and calm to your coding journey. We believe that debugging shouldn't be a frustrating experience, but an insightful process.

Our platform leverages advanced AI to provide detailed analysis, identify problems, explain causes, suggest precise solutions, and offer prevention tips for various programming languages. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting out, LogicLens aims to make your debugging process smoother and more efficient.

Our Mission

To empower developers with intelligent debugging insights, fostering a deeper understanding of code and promoting best practices.

Key Features

  • Intelligent Code Analysis powered by Gemini AI
  • Structured Debugging Reports (Summary, Problems, Causes, Solutions, Prevention)
  • Syntax-highlighted Corrected Code
  • Support for Python, Java, and JavaScript
  • User-friendly interface with a distinct aesthetic vibe
